Epoch Valentine / Air Hockey Legend

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

Air Hockey Legend collects no personal data. It has no ads, no accounts and no analytics, and it has no internet access.

Overview

This policy explains how Air Hockey Legend ("the game") handles information on Android devices. The game is published on Google Play by Epoch Valentine ("we", "us").

What the game keeps on your device

The game keeps one save file in its private storage on your device. It holds your settings (sound on or off, the screen filter), the tournament you are playing, the paddles you have unlocked and equipped, and the milestones you have reached.

  • The save file stays on your device. The game never uploads it.
  • It is not included in Android's cloud backup.
  • Uninstalling the game deletes it.

What the game does not collect

The game requests no Android permissions and has no internet access. It does not collect, send or share:

  • Personal information, such as your name, email address or phone number
  • Location
  • Device identifiers, including the advertising ID
  • Contacts, photos, files or anything else stored on your device
  • Usage analytics
  • Crash reports or diagnostics sent to us

The game contains no advertising, analytics or other third-party software that collects data.

Google Play

Google distributes the game through Google Play and collects information under its own privacy policy, for example to install and update apps. Google gives developers anonymous, aggregated statistics, such as install counts and crash rates. We do not receive information that identifies you.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal data from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If an update changes what the game collects, for example by adding optional purchases through Google Play, we will update this policy before that update is released and change the date at the top of this page.
